Port Curtis

Matthew Flinders named the harbour Port Curtis. By 1853 Francis MacCabe was surveying a town site on its shores under the protection of Native Police detachments. Before colonisation the region was home to the Gooreng Gooreng, Toolooa (Tulua), Meerooni and Baiali (Byellee) peoples. Native title around Gladstone and Bundaberg has been determined for the Bailai, Gurang, Gooreng Gooreng and Taribelang Bunda peoples (Wikipedia: Gladstone, Queensland).
